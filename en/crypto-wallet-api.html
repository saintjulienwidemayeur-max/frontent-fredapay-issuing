Crypto wallet API: create USDT and USDC wallets for your users

The Freda Pay Issuing crypto wallet API lets a fintech, a platform or a merchant create stablecoin wallets (USDT and USDC), receive and send cryptocurrency, then convert it to US dollars (USD) credited automatically to its Master Wallet.

What the crypto wallet API does

Each wallet is a deposit address for one cryptocurrency on one chain. The crypto wallet keeps its own balance, separate from your Master Wallet. You keep the funds in crypto, send them out, or convert them to dollars when you decide.

Create wallets

One POST /v1/crypto/wallets call creates the deposit address of a user or of your company for USDT or USDC on the chain you choose.

Receive and send

Receive stablecoins on the wallet address, read the balance by API, then send to an external address with an idempotency key that prevents double sends.

Convert to USD

Convert your crypto balance to dollars: the amount lands on your Master Wallet at once, ready to fund your virtual cards.

Create a crypto wallet with the API

# 1. Create a USDT wallet on Solana
curl -X POST https://api.fredapay.com/v1/crypto/wallets \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_test_YOUR_KEY" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"coin": "USDT", "chain": "solana"}'

# 2. Read the crypto balance
curl https://api.fredapay.com/v1/crypto/balance \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_test_YOUR_KEY"

# 3. Convert 100 USDT to USD into the Master Wallet
curl -X POST https://api.fredapay.com/v1/crypto/convert \
  -H "Authorization: Bearer sk_test_YOUR_KEY" \
  -H "Idempotency-Key: conv-0001" \
  -H "Content-Type: application/json" \
  -d '{"coin": "USDT", "amount": 100}'

The endpoints are /v1/crypto/wallets, /v1/crypto/balance, /v1/crypto/transactions, /v1/crypto/quote, /v1/crypto/send and /v1/crypto/convert. See the full documentation (in French) and the OpenAPI specification.

How it works

  1. You create an account and get your API key (free test mode in Sandbox).
  2. You create a wallet for one cryptocurrency and one chain: the API returns the deposit address.
  3. Your user sends USDT or USDC to that address. The crypto balance updates.
  4. You send the cryptocurrency elsewhere, or convert it to USD into your Master Wallet, with the fee breakdown calculated beforehand by /v1/crypto/quote.

Security and compliance

The provider may ask once for the identity details of the account owner to create your first wallet. They are stored securely and never returned by the API. Every send and every conversion takes an idempotency key: replaying the same request neither sends nor converts twice. Sends are irreversible: always check the address.

Frequently asked questions about the crypto wallet API

What is a crypto wallet API?

It is an interface that lets your application create cryptocurrency deposit addresses, read their balance, send funds and convert them, without running the blockchain yourself. With Freda Pay Issuing you do it with REST calls and your API key.

Which cryptocurrencies and networks are supported?

The USDT and USDC stablecoins, on Solana, Ethereum, Polygon, BSC and Tron. Sending is available on Solana for now.

How do I convert my USDT to dollars?

Call POST /v1/crypto/convert (or use the dashboard). Your crypto balance is debited and your Master Wallet is credited in USD, after the conversion rate and your plan's fee. Use /v1/crypto/quote to see the breakdown first.

Is the crypto wallet linked to the Master Wallet?

No. The crypto wallet has its own balance. It only touches the Master Wallet when you ask to convert to USD.

Can I test without real money?

Yes. With an sk_test_ key, Sandbox mode lets you try the API without real money.

What are the fees?

Deposit 0.50%. Send 2% under 6,000 and 3% above. Conversion to USD 0.70%, then your plan's fee for adding money to the Master Wallet. The exact breakdown is shown before each operation.

Who can use the crypto wallet API?

Fintechs, platforms, marketplaces and merchants with a Freda Pay Issuing account, in Haiti, Africa and worldwide. US dollar (USD) accounts for fintechs are coming soon.

Is Freda Pay Issuing a bank?

No. Freda Pay LLC is a company registered in the State of Wyoming (USA) and is not a bank. Freda Pay Issuing is not affiliated with FedaPay (fedapay.com), a different company.
